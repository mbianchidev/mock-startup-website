# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineering leaders, hiring managers, founders, and technical recruiters evaluating Matteo Bianchi for senior cloud-native, platform engineering, developer relations, or product-minded engineering roles. They arrive expecting another portfolio or resume and should quickly discover a credible builder, open-source contributor, speaker, and technical leader behind the startup parody.

## Product Purpose

Present Matteo as a product: a satirical startup platform whose features, integrations, traction, and customer proof gradually reveal a real professional track record. The experience should earn attention through humor, then convert that attention into confidence that Matteo can build useful products, communicate clearly, lead technical work, and create memorable developer experiences.

Success means a hiring company remembers the site, understands Matteo's strengths without decoding a conventional resume, explores the underlying evidence, and feels compelled to start a conversation.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, irreverent, audacious, witty, and technically formidable. The voice should be self-aware enough to parody startup theater without undermining the credibility of the work beneath it.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated SaaS landing pages with interchangeable gradients, card grids, and vague claims.
- Conventional corporate resume or portfolio sites that flatten a distinctive career into a timeline.
- Chaotic meme sites where jokes obscure navigation, evidence, or readability; memes may appear only when they sharpen the point.
- Serious enterprise consultancy sites that hide personality behind jargon, stock imagery, and institutional polish.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the bit, land with the receipts.
- Turn career evidence into product behavior instead of resume decoration.
- Make every joke carry useful information or reveal character.
- Reward curiosity with progressively stronger proof.
- Project expert confidence without taking the parody, or Matteo, too seriously.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve full keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic structure, readable contrast, and meaningful labels. Respect reduced-motion preferences and ensure all motion-enhanced content remains visible and understandable without animation.
